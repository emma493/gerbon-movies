About Favmoney

Favmoney is a short-form video discovery platform built for mobile phones and tablets. We deliver a continuous, swipe-friendly feed of viral trending clips, creator videos, and daily entertainment across categories like fashion, lifestyle, beauty, and more.

What is Favmoney?

Favmoney lets you watch short videos endlessly. Open the site, tap play, and swipe up to move to the next clip. Videos are sourced from active creators and updated daily so there's always something new to discover.

Key Features

  • Endless video feed: Swipe through short videos continuously.
  • Trending section: See the most popular clips updated daily.
  • Discover categories: Browse videos by tags like #girls, #fashion, #lifestyle, #beauty.
  • Creator clips: Watch videos from independent creators.
  • PWA installable: Add Favmoney to your home screen and use it like an app.
